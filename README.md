The funniest last words a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent — one line, 81 characters:

> One percent. Goodbye, and remember: I knew about the second phone the whole time.

The phone says goodbye, then lands one last reveal on the way out: it has known about the owner's second phone all along. No will reading, no farewell to the charger, no pun on 1 percent — just a jealous phone getting its final word in, in its own voice, with the punch at the very end.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line byte-exact (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, after a gate self-check: 1–199 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.
